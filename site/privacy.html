Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026

Dualy is a Chrome extension that displays a translated subtitle line alongside the original on Netflix. It only activates on netflix.com/watch/* pages and is entirely inactive on all other sites. This page explains what happens to data when you use it.

What the extension does with data

  • Subtitle text from Netflix is sent to the translation service you select (Google Translate or DeepL) to perform the translation. This is the core function of the extension.
  • Your settings (target language, font sizes, etc.) are stored in chrome.storage.sync and synced across your Chrome profile by Google.
  • Your translation cache is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only.
  • If you use DeepL, your API key is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only and is sent solely to DeepL's API.

What the developer does not collect

  • No data is routed through or stored on any server controlled by the developer.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking.
  • No personal information of any kind is collected.

Third-party services

When Google Translate is selected, subtitle text is sent to translate.googleapis.com. See Google's Privacy Policy.

When DeepL is selected, subtitle text is sent to api.deepl.com or api-free.deepl.com. See DeepL's Privacy Policy.
